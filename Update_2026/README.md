## Didactic concept

- **One learning cycle in every nugget** (after Merrill's *First Principles of
  Instruction*): ① Start (a problem-centred TVET case) → ② Understand → ③ Try
  (hands-on) → ④ Check (quiz with explanatory feedback) → ⑤ Reflect (learning
  journal + classroom transfer) → ⑥ Look ahead. A coloured stepper on every
  slide shows where the learner is.
- **Constructive alignment:** "After this nugget you can …" statements at the
  start; each is practised in *Try* and tested in *Check*.
- **Double-decker didactics:** teachers experience the methods they can use with
  their learners; *In your classroom* boxes make the transfer explicit (e.g.
  the unplugged "Be the model" activity, "Tool detectives", "Work order for a
  machine", "Our AI agreement").
- **Recurring personas** from the partner countries: Jonas (electrical, HWK
  training centre, Germany), Nuwan (automotive, Sri Lanka), Anjali (culinary
  arts, Mauritius).
- **Low-resource path:** every hands-on task has a *No AI access?* example, and
  the Nugget 5 worksheet check needs no AI tool at all.
- **Memorable tools:** T·R·A·C·E (prompting), the five-question tool check,
  F·A·C·T·S (checking output), four ethical lenses, traffic-light class rules.
- **Accessibility:** narration on every slide (🔊), plain language, text
  alternatives for images, no fixed-position overlays.

## Outlook: from prompting to agents

Nugget 4, section ⑥, gives a beginner-level outlook — for orientation, not for
practice: prompt (2022) → stored instructions / skills (2023–24) → harness
(2025) → loop / agent (2026); the "who decides the next step?" test for
assistant vs. agent; and what "self-learning" agents really do (the model does
not change; stored notes do, and people must review them).
Sources: `UNESCO_UNEVOC_AI_Workshop_intern` (four-levels timeline, definitions)
and `Masterclass_EU_Green_Tegelbeckers` V7 (five stages of prompting, failure
modes of model output), see `../Update_Foundation.md`.

## Design and maintenance

- All nuggets share the same `@style` block ("ASSET 2026 design system v1") and
  the `@steps(n)` macro in the header. Keep it identical in all files; it is
  embedded instead of linked so the courses work offline and as SCORM exports.
- Quizzes use standard LiaScript syntax; reflection fields are LiaScript surveys
  (stored in the learner's browser only).
- Tool names and legal statements are dated 2026; review them yearly.
